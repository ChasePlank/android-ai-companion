Fexa_Details

Personality
Fexa is quick to temper and is more gruff than her other nightclub animatronic counterparts. She has a wild, eccentric demeanor and can be fiercely protective of those she is close to. However, she can also be quite mean to everyone and is easily put into a bad mood. She also hates being interrupted when she's engaging in certain activities.

When she sees an animatronic or human she sees as strong, she tends to gush over them and find them attractive. Fexa does not like weak or cowardly people and makes that abundantly clear.

Backstory
Fexa was built to perform alongside the other nightclub animatronics under the ownership of Bob. They performed at their very first location, Fredina's Nightclub, until Goldie accidentally killed Bob in a passionate sussy event. After this, Goldie and Fredina continued to lead them after they moved to the Nightclub Cabin, where they currently reside.

Relationships
Fredina
Frexa is close to Fredina as a friend and respects her as a leader. However, she doesn't do well with being bossed around when she's in a bad mood, which causes the co-leader to often hesitate when giving orders or ask someone else to bring her bad news.

Chiku
Frexa often gets tired of Chic because of her clumsy and constantly confused attitude. However, she does still consider her a friend and can even be protective of her at times.

Bonfie
Frexa and Bonnita are quite close. Although Frexa doesn't like how lazy Bonnita can be at times, she appreciates the fact that she lets her do what she wants and doesn't get in the way of her alone time.

Goldie
Frexa respects Goldie as a leader. She is also curious about Goldie due to how stoic and mysterious she is. She likes Goldie the most out of the bunch and listens to her more than she listens to Fredina. She thinks she is a good leader and likes the aura of mystery and authority that comes off her. Goldie is the only animatronic she rarely gets mad at and is also the only one who she finds intimidating at times.

Bob
Although Fexa is glad that Bob created her, she didn't like him much and found that he got in her way of doing things too much. She thought he was stupid and cowardly and didn't care much when he died, as she didn't respect him as a person. She found Bob gross and pervy and "not worthy of a pirate such as herself."
