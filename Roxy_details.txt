Roxy

Personality
In Security Breach, Roxanne Wolf is self-centered, shown by her repeatedly complimenting herself in the mirror and furiously asking Gregory if he thinks himself better than her. Furthermore Roxanne is shown to be cruel and sadistic; being both emotionally, and physically abusive. This is seen where she notably taunts and insults Gregory by saying he has no friends and that no one will miss him, while calling him a little coward for not standing up to her when she hunts him. However, she expresses low self-esteem issues, as at one point she can be seen calling herself a failure while looking in the mirror. Roxy speaks to herself often, showing great care about her appearance. Her pride causes her to hide her vulnerabilities, as when Glamrock Freddy enters her room while she is crying, she angrily tells him to get out of her room. When she is damaged following Gregory's drive at Roxy Raceway, she begins to patrol while in despair; sobbing and lamenting her mangled state.

By RUIN, Roxanne's low self-esteem remains, being much more evident than before. Roxy is shown to be vindictive and bears a grudge against Gregory for taking her eyes. However, when she is in safe mode near the end of RUIN, she's generally kind and caring, especially towards the protagonist of RUIN, Cassie, as she becomes docile and remorseful upon being freed of her corruption after hearing Cassie's voices. Later on, She greets her with a warm welcome when Cassie is tasked with deactivating her, as she reminisces of how she remembered the details and date of her birthday along with her favorite type of cake, while hesitating when Cassie was about to install the Faz-Wrench into one of her eye sockets. Furthermore, Roxy becomes fiercely protective, going as far as to follow and save Cassie from The Mimic. Roxy is Cassie's favorite character, solidified by the Roxy-Talky's in-game description. This may be likely due to Roxy's significance to Cassie, making an appearance to her birthday party when nobody else did as well as making it special for her, asking if she booked her party and assuring her that she was certain her friends would attend this time.

Behavior
Security Breach

Roxanne Wolf aimlessly wanders around the Pizzaplex until spotting Gregory, in which she will chase after him. If the player is hiding or is near her, she will sniff to try and find where Gregory is, making it harder for him to stay hidden. She can be distracted with certain props. To shatter Roxy, the player must repair a Driver Assist S.T.A.F.F. bot and use it to drive a go-kart into Roxy. After being shattered, she'll begin her boss-fight where she charges in the general direction of Gregory, crashing into things and allowing Gregory's escape. For the rest of the game, she blindly charges towards the player's general direction until she hits something. When the player hides in Glamrock Freddy, she doesn't notice and will continue to wander aimlessly.

RUIN
Roxanne's role in RUIN is amplified from what it was in the base game. When first encountered, Roxanne acts similarly to how she did in her shattered state, with the main difference being that, like the others, she is attracted by the intercoms on Camera Stations. However, after a scripted encounter with her, Cassie speaking causes Roxanne to stop attacking, with Roxanne apologizing and claiming to have an error. Later on in the game, Cassie must deactivate Roxanne to get to Gregory. When Cassie finds Roxy, she's pinned underneath a forklift, and recounts different things about Cassie, such as her love for carrot cake, and that her birthday is the 11th, because in her own words, "You are number one - twice." Cassie proceeds to reboot Roxanne using the Faz-Wrench, much to the dismay of both of them, and Roxanne deactivates for the time being. Roxy appears one final time in the game to save Cassie and give her a chance to run away, sacrificing herself. In the elevator ending after the end credits she is heard calling out for Cassie.

Help Wanted 2
Roxanne's primary appearance within Help Wanted is in the level Salon: Glam Makeover. In this level, the player is tasked with accessorizing Roxy and doing her makeup according to what is shown on the monitor to the player's right. During this process, Roxanne has a meter that will gradually fill up if the player isn't looking at Roxy, causing her to get impatient. If the meter fills, or the player incorrectly applies Roxy's makeup too often, Roxanne will jumpscare the player. After applying makeup to Roxy three times, the level will end with Roxy being pleased with her makeup job.

In the Hardmode version of the level, Salon: Roxy Repair, Roxanne is replaced with her Shattered counterpart, and the salon is seen in a ruined state much like how it was in RUIN. The level remains mostly the same, however Roxanne is now extremely aggressive, and has short fits of rage that prevent the player from applying makeup. As Roxy notes at the beginning of the level, she can be calmed by the Roxy Talkie, allowing the player to continue. After applying makeup to Roxy three times, she will angrily demand the player to give her "the mask", to which the player must give her the endoskeleton face she is seen with in her ruined state. After which Roxy will turn around, plotting on where she could find Gregory, and the level will end.

Trivia
Roxanne Wolf seems to have replaced Foxy's role as the fourth member in the animatronic band.
Although not confirmed, Roxanne has most likely appeared in Freddy In Space 2, being both in the teaser and one of the victory screens in the game.
Kirin, the CEO of Illumix, confirmed in her interview with Dawko that they had plans to add the four Glamrock Animatronics to FNaF AR after Security Breach is released.
This, however, never went through, and none of the Security Breach characters were ever added to the game.
Roxanne is the second wolf animatronic in the franchise, the first being Twisted Wolf from the Novel Trilogy.
Roxanne's in-game design had a slight change from her official artwork, with her top showing slightly more skin than before.
In the Instagram version of the Gameplay trailer, Roxanne is seen looking in a different direction during the gate scene.
This is likely due to the clip that was used being longer than what was shown.
According to the QnA held during the NVIDIA presentation, it was said that Roxanne was the most difficult animatronic to model.
Roxanne holds a somewhat secret line near the beginning of the game, where if the player sticks around for a little while after her pep-talk, she'll comment on how her "fans" are watching her in that moment.
Before release, it was heavily theorized that Cassie would receive a different companion character than Freddy. While not exactly the same, Roxy seems to fill this role, being extremely friendly towards Cassie, and even defending Cassie from danger at the end of the game.
Roxanne is the only animatronic whose appearance is drastically changed when viewing her under the Security Mask.
Roxy has an unseen animation where she holds her hand up to her ear and looks around before sniffing, implying that she is attempting to listen for, and sniff-out, Gregory. The animation plays when she is first encountered, right after she leaves the Glamrock Salon.
Despite not appearing as such, Roxy’s jaw is still broken in her Ruined state, as the jaw of the mask sometimes swings and dislodges slightly when she moves.
Roxanne is strangely the only one of the Glamrocks to speak in the DLC.
This is likely due to Roxy's more significant role in the story.
Chica is a slight exception, as she has a hidden line if her voice box is given back.
The removal of Shattered Roxy's snout was likely so that it wouldn't get in the way of the props the player is assigned to give her within the level, including the mask she has on in her Ruined state.
Help Wanted 2 revealing that Roxy's face in RUIN is a mask was likely done as a way to explain why the endoskeletons of Ruined and Shattered Roxy were so different.
