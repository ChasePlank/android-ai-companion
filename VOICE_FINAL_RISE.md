A CPU Wav2Vec2 ASR sanity check must recognize ARE YOU LOST. CTC forced alignment estimates the final-word interval and must pass token/score checks. Torch/TorchAudio are pinned to 2.8.0 because these alignment APIs were removed in later versions. The official alignment checkpoint is loaded with weights_only=True; its SHA-256 is recorded after download, not independently pre-pinned. Model download is approximately 360 MB, plus CPU package dependencies. No remote speech service receives the audio.

Praat extracts a pitch tier. The first three local voiced pitch points set an onset anchor; only positive pitch excursion above it is compressed to nominally 75% or 50%, with a 3-semitone cut cap and smooth 30-ms boundary transitions. PSOLA overlap-add resynthesis is spliced inside the estimated final-word region, with local peak attenuation if necessary. Actual sample preservation outside that region and unchanged total sample count are checked. No automatic claim is made that the estimated interval precisely matches human word boundaries or that the audible rise equals the nominal setting. Unclear alignment, insufficient pitch points, likely octave jumps or no positive rise stop the test rather than guessing.

This is post-synthesis baseline editing, not a new Piper prosody capability, accent training, emotion control, or RVC modification. A successful numeric check does not establish naturalness or likeness; resynthesis may introduce artifacts. The app, avatars, ONNX and existing tests remain untouched. Artifacts expire after seven days. Read-only repository/action permissions, CPU/network downloads, normal Actions usage and a 30-minute limit apply. Existing licensing/provenance constraints remain unresolved.
